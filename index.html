What We Offer

Opportunities that move you forward

From short summer programs to dual-degree pathways — we open doors for students, researchers, and partner institutions.

Student Exchange

Semester and year-long exchange opportunities with partner universities across Asia, Europe, and beyond.

Scholarships

Funding for international study, research internships, and travel grants for engineering students.

Joint Research

Collaborative research projects, dual-degree options, and visiting scholar arrangements with global faculty.

Summer Schools

Short-term intensive programs in engineering, sustainability, and emerging technologies — open to international applicants.

Inbound Programs
