About SET International Affairs

We are the school-level International Affairs unit of the School of Engineering and Technology (SET) — building bridges to the world for our six engineering departments.

School level, university wide

SET International Affairs is the international arm of the School of Engineering and Technology. We work hand-in-hand with Walailak University's Center for International Affairs (CIA) — the university-level office — while driving the school's own MOUs, double-degree programs, Erasmus+ projects, and staff & student mobility.

WU Center for International Affairs ↗  ·  SET Official Website ↗

Our Mission

To globalize engineering education at Walailak University by enabling meaningful international experiences for every student, fostering world-class research collaboration, and welcoming global talent to our campus.

Our Vision

To be the most outward-facing engineering school in Southern Thailand — recognized for the quality of its international partnerships, mobility programs, and joint research output.

What We Value

These principles guide how we work with students, faculty, and partners.

Opportunity for all

Every engineering student deserves access to international experiences — we work to remove financial, language, and information barriers.

Strong partnerships

We build long-term, two-way relationships with partner institutions, not one-off exchanges.

Quality & impact

We measure success not by paperwork signed, but by student outcomes, research output, and lasting relationships.

Cultural openness

We celebrate diversity and build a campus environment where international students thrive.
